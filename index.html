About Me

I am a student composer and erhu practitioner. My work sits at the intersection of folk music, original composition, and music therapy research. I lead Erhu Music Planet, an international community sharing Chinese erhu music globally, including outreach for deaf and hard-of-hearing learners. My senior project investigates AI-assisted calming preoperative music for children, using EEG physiological markers to compare human-composed and AI-generated sound.

Outside music, I practice figure skating and bake tiramisu, both teaching me patience and careful precision.

Erhu & Community Outreach

Erhu Music Planet, Co-founder

I built this global online community to introduce erhu and Chinese folk music to worldwide audiences. Members include folk music lovers, diaspora communities, and people who have never touched a string instrument. We run workshops for deaf and mute learners, exploring music through vibration and physical rhythm beyond sound.

Senior Research: Music Therapy

AI-Assisted Preoperative Calming Music Generation for School-Aged Children, guided by EEG markers

This project tests whether custom music reduces preoperative anxiety in children. I created a human-composed musical piece, paired with an AI-generated counterpart. Working with Professor Qin Yi, our team uses EEG and physiological measurements to compare responses to both forms of music.

Research question: What differences exist between human-composed and algorithm-generated calming sound in easing children’s fear before surgery?
